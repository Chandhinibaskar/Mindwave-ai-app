About Mindwave

Built to make emotional self-awareness a five-minute daily habit, not a clinical chore.

Our approach

We combine NLP emotion detection with evidence-informed self-care practices — breathing, journaling, and trend awareness — to help you notice patterns early.

Not a replacement

Mindwave supports reflection. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and always defers to licensed professionals and crisis services for urgent needs.

Privacy first

Anonymous mode, encrypted storage, and no sharing of personal journal content — your data stays yours.

Contact & FAQ

Is my data private? +
Yes. Anonymous mode and encrypted storage keep your entries private by default.
Is this a diagnosis tool? +
No. Mindwave offers reflective insight, not medical diagnosis. Please consult a professional for clinical concerns.
What languages are supported? +
The interface supports English, Spanish, and Hindi, with more planned.
Can I export my mood history? +
Yes, from the Dashboard you can export a PDF mood report anytime.
